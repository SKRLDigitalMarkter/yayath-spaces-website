What to Look for in a Coworking Space

Not all coworking spaces are created equal. When evaluating options, prioritize these factors:

  • Internet Reliability: Dedicated leased lines with 99.9% uptime SLA are non-negotiable.
  • Power Backup: 24/7 diesel generator + UPS backup ensures zero downtime.
  • Security: Biometric/smart card access, 24/7 CCTV, and professional security personnel.
  • Community and Networking: Regular events, a diverse member base, and community management teams.
  • Scalability: Can the space grow with you? Can you add seats or move to a larger private office at the same provider?
