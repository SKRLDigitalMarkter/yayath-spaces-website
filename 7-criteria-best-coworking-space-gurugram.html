Criterion 1: Location and Commute Accessibilityaa

Location is the single most important factor when selecting a coworking space in Gurugram. A great office in an inconvenient location will frustrate your team and drive up attrition.

Ask yourself these questions:

  • Is the coworking centre within walking distance of a Rapid Metro or Delhi Metro station?
  • Does it have adequate parking for team members who drive?
  • Is it on a key commercial corridor like MG Road, Golf Course Road, Golf Course Extension Road, or Sohna Road?
  • Are there restaurants, banks, ATMs, and retail outlets nearby?

Criterion 2: Internet Reliability and Technical Infrastructure

For a modern business, internet downtime is lost revenue. When evaluating any office space for rent in Gurugram, do not just take the advertised speed at face value. Dig deeper.

  • Is it a dedicated leased line or shared broadband? Dedicated leased lines guarantee your bandwidth regardless of how many people are online.
  • Is there a failover connection? A second ISP as backup ensures zero downtime even if the primary connection fails.
  • What is the uptime SLA? Ask for a written commitment of 99.9% uptime.
  • Is there enterprise-grade Wi-Fi? Look for access points every 10-15 workstations, not a single router for the floor.

At Yayath Spaces, every centre is equipped with dual-ISP dedicated leased lines, enterprise Wi-Fi with full coverage, and a 99.9% uptime guarantee backed by an SLA.

Criterion 3: Power Backup and Business Continuity

Gurugram, like many Indian cities, can experience power fluctuations — especially during summer months. For businesses running critical operations, power cuts are not just inconvenient; they can lead to data loss and missed client deadlines.

Before signing up for any managed office space in Gurugram, confirm:

  • Is there 100% power backup via diesel generator?
  • Is there also a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) for instant failover during switchover?
  • Are all server rooms and critical infrastructure on dedicated UPS lines?

At Yayath Spaces, every location has 100% power backup via diesel DG sets, combined with UPS for seamless, zero-interruption power during transitions.

Criterion 4: Space Types, Scalability, and Flexibility

Your business needs will change. A great coworking space in Gurugram must be able to grow with you — not trap you in a configuration that no longer fits.

Evaluate these space types and options:

  • Hot Desks: Ideal for freelancers and solo professionals who need flexibility.
  • Dedicated Desks: Your own permanent seat in a shared area. Perfect for consistent remote workers.
  • Private Cabins: Enclosed spaces for teams of 2-10 who need focus and privacy.
  • Enterprise Managed Floors: Custom-configured, fully branded floors for large teams of 50-200+.
  • Meeting Rooms: On-demand bookable conference rooms with AV setups and whiteboards.

The key question to ask is: Can I easily move from a 5-seat cabin to a 20-seat office within the same provider without moving buildings? At Yayath Spaces, the answer is always yes.

Criterion 5: Amenities and Daily Comfort

Your team spends 8-10 hours a day in this space. The quality of daily amenities directly impacts productivity, morale, and retention. When evaluating private office space in Gurugram, check for:

  • Fully stocked pantry with premium tea, coffee, and refreshments
  • 24/7 air conditioning with smart temperature controls
  • Clean, well-maintained washrooms
  • Ergonomic furniture (chairs and desks that support long work sessions)
  • Printing, scanning, and photocopying facilities
  • Reception and concierge services
  • Lockers and secure storage
  • Breakout lounge areas for informal collaboration

Do not compromise on these fundamentals. A coworking space that cuts corners on amenities will cost you in employee dissatisfaction.

When choosing office space for rent in Gurugram, security is non-negotiable — especially for businesses handling sensitive client data or confidential intellectual property.

  • Professional Security Personnel: Trained security staff at the building entrance and on your floor.
  • Visitor Management System: Digital visitor logs with photo ID verification.
