Dark Stores

High-density, hyper-local micro-fulfillment centers driving India's quick-commerce revolution. Enable 10-minute deliveries by positioning your inventory closer to the consumer.

Enquire Now

What is a Dark Store?

A dark store is a micro-fulfilment center located in high-density urban areas. Unlike a traditional warehouse which sits on the outskirts of the city, a dark store is right in the neighborhood. It is not open to the public; it is dedicated purely to rapid online order processing.

Traditional Warehouse vs Dark Store

  • Size: Traditional (100k+ sq. ft.) vs Dark Store (2,500 - 5,000 sq. ft.)
  • SKUs: Traditional (Bulk pallets) vs Dark Store (3,000 to 5,000 high-velocity SKUs)
  • Fulfillment Time: Traditional (24-48 hours) vs Dark Store (Under 10 minutes)

Inside the Dark Store: Layout & Zones

Optimized for speed. Every second counts in a 10-minute delivery model.

Inbound/Receiving Zone

Where mother-hub trucks drop off daily inventory. Goods are rapidly scanned and sorted into totes.

Ambient Zone

Racks for FMCG, snacks, cosmetics, and electronics. Organized by velocity (fastest moving items closest to the packing station).

Chiller & Freezer Zone

Temperature-controlled areas (0-4°C and -18°C) for dairy, fresh produce, meat, and frozen goods.

Picking Aisles

Narrow aisles designed for pickers with handheld scanners and wearable devices to collect 10-15 items per minute.

Packing & Dispatch

Orders are bagged, tagged, and handed directly to delivery riders waiting outside the dispatch window.

How Do Dark Stores Make Money?

Successful dark stores run on high order volumes (1,000 to 2,000 orders per day per store). Revenue is generated through retail margins, delivery fees, brand advertising (in-app placements), and surge pricing. Profitability is achieved when the density of orders in a 2-km radius crosses a critical threshold, optimizing rider utilization.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do I need for a Dark Store?

Typically between 2,500 and 5,000 sq. ft., depending on the SKU count (usually 3,000–5,000 SKUs).

Do I need specialized software?

Yes, standard WMS will not work. You need a micro-fulfillment operating system capable of batch picking, dynamic slotting, and rider assignment in seconds.

What are the power requirements?

High power backup is essential. Chillers and freezers must run 24/7 without interruption.

Cold Storage

Temperature-controlled warehousing for perishables, pharmaceuticals, and sensitive goods. Zero temperature breaks. 100% compliance.

Request Cold Chain Quote

Multi-Chamber Temperature Control

Not all products are equal. We offer segregated chambers designed for specific temperature ranges to ensure maximum shelf-life and product integrity.

Frozen (-18°C to -25°C)

Ideal for frozen foods, meat, poultry, seafood, and specific frozen pharma goods like vaccines.

Chilled (2°C to 8°C)

Perfect for dairy, fresh fruits, vegetables, baked goods, and standard pharmaceutical products.

Air-Conditioned (15°C to 25°C)

Controlled ambient storage for chocolates, cosmetics, supplements, and certain medical devices.

Engineered for Compliance & Security

WHO-GDP & FSSAI Compliant

Our facilities meet the highest regulatory standards for pharmaceutical (Good Distribution Practices) and food safety (FSSAI) storage.

100% Power Redundancy

Dual-backup DG sets and advanced UPS systems guarantee zero downtime. Your temperature logs will never show a spike.

IoT Monitoring & Datalogging

Cloud-connected sensors track temperature and humidity 24/7. Get automated alerts via SMS/Email if parameters deviate by even 0.5°C.

The Processing Flow

  1. Docking: Trucks dock at insulated bays with inflatable dock shelters to prevent outside air from entering.
  2. Pre-Cooling: Goods are staged in an ante-room (airlock) for temperature stabilization and quality checking.
  3. Putaway: Using reach trucks, pallets are stored in high-density ASRS or drive-in racking inside the designated thermal chamber.
  4. Dispatch: FEFO (First Expiry, First Out) rules apply. Goods are picked and loaded directly into reefer trucks via the insulated dock.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get historical temperature logs for compliance?

Yes, our 21 CFR Part 11 compliant data loggers provide exportable, tamper-proof audit trails for any time period.

Do you provide reefer transportation?

Yes, we offer primary and secondary cold-chain transport (reefer trucks) for an unbroken cold chain from factory to warehouse to distributor.

Are your panels fire-rated?

Our facility uses PIR (Polyisocyanurate) insulated sandwich panels which offer superior fire resistance and thermal efficiency compared to standard EPS panels.
