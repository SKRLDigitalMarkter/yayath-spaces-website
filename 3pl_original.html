What is 3PL?

Third-party logistics (3PL) allows businesses to outsource their warehousing and logistics operations to an external specialist. While you own the inventory and focus on selling the product, we manage the physical supply-chain activities.

From receiving goods and quality checks to order processing, picking, packing, and returns—we handle it all efficiently.

How It Works

1. Inbound & Verify

Customer sends inventory. We receive, verify quality/quantity, and record in WMS.

2. Allocate & Store

Products are intelligently allocated to racks or bins for optimal retrieval speeds.

3. Order Fulfillment

Customer orders stream in. Pickers collect, verify, pack securely, and label.

4. Dispatch & Analytics

Carrier collects shipments. Customer receives live inventory and performance reports.
